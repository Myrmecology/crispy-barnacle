Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial? +
Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You get full access to every feature during the trial period.
How does pricing work? +
We charge per seat, per month. Starter plans begin at $29/seat. Enterprise pricing is custom — reach out to our sales team for a quote.
Can I self-host NovaTech? +
Self-hosted deployments are available on our Enterprise plan. We provide full Docker images and support for on-premise installations.
What integrations are supported? +
NovaTech integrates with Slack, GitHub, Jira, Linear, Zapier, and over 200 other tools via our REST API and native connectors.
