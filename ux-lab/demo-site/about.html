// What We Stand For

Our core values.

01

Clarity over complexity

Every feature we ship must make things simpler, not more complicated. If it adds confusion, it does not ship.

02

Speed is a feature

We treat performance as a core product value, not an afterthought. Fast products respect your users' time.

03

Radical transparency

We share what we are building, why we are building it, and when we get things wrong. No spin, no fluff.

04

Users first, always

Every product decision starts with one question — does this make our users more successful? If not, we pass.
